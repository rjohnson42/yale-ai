Sierra conversational AI platform

A platform for businesses to deploy AI agents that handle customer experiences and pursue business outcomes.

Unifies customer interactions and outcomes across channels, with built-in trust, security, compliance, and guardrails.

Ghostwriter

An agent-building agent that creates and optimizes production-ready customer-experience agents from prompts, SOPs, transcripts, audio interviews, or other business materials.

Lets customer-experience teams build, test, review, and continuously improve multilingual, multichannel agents without clicks, code, or complex manual debugging.

Agent Studio

A no-code environment for building and managing agents, configuring customer journeys and knowledge, defining brand guidelines, and running simulations.

Empowers customer-experience teams to manage agents without writing code.

Horizon

Long-horizon agents that act on signals, retain customer context, plan next steps, and orchestrate inbound and outbound interactions over days, weeks, or months.

Drives high-value outcomes through proactive, omnichannel engagement, persistent memory, next-best-action decisioning, and playbooks.

Insights

Analytics, experimentation, monitoring, auditing, and observability for evaluating and optimizing agent performance.

Helps businesses track metrics such as CSAT and resolution, investigate trends, identify conversations needing attention, run experiments, and understand agent actions.

Explorer

Natural-language analytics and conversation research that answers questions about customer conversations and recommends improvements.

Provides ChatGPT-style analysis and actionable recommendations from agent and customer-interaction data.

Multichannel agent deployment

A single agent can operate across chat, SMS, WhatsApp, email, voice, and ChatGPT.

Unifies channels and preserves context as customers switch between them.

Context Engine

Customer-context and memory capabilities used to connect conversations, systems, and touchpoints.

Enables personalized decisions and continuity across customer journeys.

Why they fit

HCA is an exceptionally strong enterprise fit for Sierra: it operates 190 hospitals and approximately 2,500 ambulatory sites, supports 47 million patient encounters annually, and already has an Automation Center of Excellence plus patient-experience and workflow-transformation programs. Sierra could complement—not replace—HCA's internal automation by orchestrating complex patient-access, scheduling, registration, billing, and follow-up journeys across channels with stronger conversational context, guardrails, auditability, and measurable outcomes.

Business context

HCA's Information Technology Group serves a large, distributed care network and explicitly discusses automation, patient-information automation, call/text analysis, automated emergency-room registration, EHR modernization, and care transformation. This suggests sophisticated technical and operational buyers, as well as complex regulated workflows where Sierra's trust and governance positioning matters.
