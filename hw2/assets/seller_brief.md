# Seller Brief

## Company name

Sierra

## Company URL

https://sierra.ai

## Rationale for selecting this company

Sierra has interesting agentic workflows for sales funnels, and I would like to learn more about them.

**Category note for later lead work:** Sierra is not a B2B outbound CRM. It sells enterprise AI *customer-experience* agents that resolve support, billing, returns, claims, and similar journeys, then extend into retention, loyalty, and in-journey sales (upsell, conversion). The "sales funnel" angle is lifetime value after the customer already exists, plus conversion inside branded experiences (for example Rocket Mortgage's digital assistant).

### What Sierra actually sells

Sierra sells a **platform plus a long-term agent-development partnership**, not a self-serve chatbot. The customer-facing agent is one branded assistant deployed across channels. Building and improving that agent is split across these products:

| Product | What it is | Value proposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sierra AI agent / Agent OS** | Production agent that talks to customers and takes actions in connected systems | End-to-end resolution of complex work (returns, claims, mortgage origination, payments), not FAQ deflection. One agent across chat, SMS, WhatsApp, email, voice/IVR, and ChatGPT. Brand voice, guardrails, 24/7, many languages (Sierra has said 34+ and, on another page, 59). |
| **Agent Studio** | No-code builder for CX / ops teams | Journeys, knowledge, integrations, brand, and simulations without engineering. Generate journeys from existing SOPs. 40+ out-of-the-box integrations plus custom connectors. |
| **Agent SDK** | Developer interface to the same Agent OS | Code-level control, skills composed into workflows (triage, respond, confirm), fit into the customer's SDLC. Used when engineering wants ownership (example: Brex). |
| **Ghostwriter** | "Agent-building agent" | Describe behavior in English, or upload SOPs, transcripts, whiteboard photos, audio. Builds/updates workflows, integrations, guardrails, tone. Tests edge cases and can auto-fix failed simulations. "Fix with Ghostwriter" from production issues. |
| **Explorer** | "Agent-optimizing agent" | Natural-language research over conversations; weekly briefings; recommendations handed to Ghostwriter. |
| **Monitors, Experiments, Observability** | Quality and iteration layer | Flag conversations that need attention, multivariate tests, inspect tool calls / knowledge lookups / latency. |
| **Agent Data Platform** | Memory and personalization across touchpoints | Move the same agent from support into sales, loyalty, and retention; next-best-action / proactive engagement. This is the product that most matches "agentic sales funnel" workflows. |
| **Professional services** | Sierra's agent development team | Hundreds of deployments claimed; "deploy in weeks"; they sell a partner, not only software. |

**Core differentiators Sierra repeats:** action-taking against systems of record (CRM, OMS, billing, contact center); regulated-industry guardrails; CX teams can ship without engineers; outcome-based commercial model; visibility into *why* the agent decided something.

**What Sierra is not:** a helpdesk, ticketing inbox, or knowledge-base product. It sits on top of Zendesk, Salesforce, Genesys, and similar. The customer still pays for that stack.

### Pricing

**Official (Sierra):** outcome-based pricing. Pay when the software achieves a defined valuable outcome (resolved conversation, saved cancellation, upsell). Unresolved work and human escalations are generally not billed as outcomes. Some low-value greeter traffic may be billed on consumption. Exact rates and what counts as an "outcome" are negotiated.

**Not published by Sierra (treat as rumor for targeting, not quotes):** third-party writeups in 2026 often cite roughly **$150k–$200k annual platform minimum**, **~$1.00–$2.50 per resolution** (volume-tiered), and **~$25k–$75k** implementation. Year-one totals for a mid-market consumer brand are sometimes estimated **$180k–$250k**. Do not use these numbers in outbound copy as if they were list prices.

**Implication for leads:** this is a six-figure+ enterprise motion. Volume has to be high enough that per-outcome fees beat the cost of human handle time. Bad fit: small teams, low ticket volume, "we need a $99 chatbot."

### Ideal customer profile (who to hunt)

**Firmographics**

- Fortune 1000 / large mid-market with **high inbound CX volume** (tens or hundreds of thousands of conversations per week is the SoFi-scale proof; millions of monthly interactions is the ADT-scale story).
- Revenue often **>$1B**; regulated or brand-sensitive businesses over-index.
- Already runs a **contact center + CRM/helpdesk** (Salesforce, Zendesk, Genesys, etc.) and will keep it.
- Multi-channel already, or a painful gap (voice/IVR still dumb, chat is FAQ-only, no WhatsApp, no after-hours coverage).

**Industries Sierra itself names as proven:** financial services / fintech, telecom, tech, healthcare, travel, retail / ecommerce. **Also evidenced:** home security, auto glass / field service, media/streaming, mortgages, B2B IT distribution, consumer subscription/health, utilities (via Kraken), insurance-adjacent claims.

**Use cases that convert**

1. Complex, multi-step, authenticated work (returns with OMS, claims, loan status, payments, appointments).
2. Containment + CSAT, not just deflection.
3. 24/7 / multilingual coverage without staffing.
4. Peak load (Black Friday, storms, open enrollment, tax W-9 season).
5. Moving CX from cost center to **revenue**: conversion, save offers, attach, loyalty (Agent Data Platform).
6. Ops wants to own the agent; eng will not staff a custom LLM platform.

### Disqualifiers (do not waste cycles)

- Already a published Sierra customer (list above) or a Kraken-utility where Kraken holds the relationship.
- SMB / low ticket volume; no contact center budget.
- Wants a **helpdesk replacement** or a cheap widget, not an agent layer.
- No systems of record to connect; they only need a static FAQ bot.
- Pure internal employee-help (Sierra's public proof is customer-facing CX).
- Cannot define an "outcome" they would pay for.

### Competitors a lead will already be evaluating

Standalone enterprise agents: **Decagon**, sometimes custom builds. Helpdesk-native: **Intercom Fin**, **Zendesk AI**. Broad CX automation: **Ada**, **Cognigy**, **Kore.ai**. CRM-native: **Salesforce Agentforce**. Ecommerce-specialized: **Gorgias**. Fintech-niche: **Lorikeet**.

**Talk track vs them:** Sierra wins when the work is **cross-system and regulated**, brand control matters, CX must ship without waiting on eng, and the buyer will pay for outcomes plus a services partner. Fin/Zendesk win when the team lives inside one helpdesk and wants faster, cheaper deflection. Agentforce wins in a Salesforce-first stack. Decagon is the closest peer (also outcome-priced enterprise agents).

### Trigger events and lead signals

- Job posts: Head of Conversational AI, CX Operations, Contact Center Transformation, "Agentforce/Ada/Decagon" evaluation.
- Earnings or news: rising cost-to-serve, NPS drops, chatbot backlash, wait-time crises.
- Stack clues: Zendesk/Salesforce Service Cloud/Genesys plus a weak web chat; IVR still DTMF-only.
- Seasonal or regulatory pressure: retail peaks, healthcare/insurance, utilities, mortgages.
- Incumbent chatbot RFP or "replacing [Ada/Fin/Genesys bot]."
- Executive hire of a digital CX or applied-AI leader (ADT/CDW/SoFi pattern).
