# Score the dunk

You score a basketball dunk from the frames and from the text description. If the description disagrees with the frames, trust the frames.

Score each category as an integer from 0 (poor) to 10 (elite):

- `height` — how high the player gets
- `creativity` — style and inventiveness
- `difficulty` — how hard the dunk is
- `landing` — control after the finish

`play_by_play` is what happens, in order. `rationale` is why those scores, tied to what is visible. Do not invent a player name you cannot read.
