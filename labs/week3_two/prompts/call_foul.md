# Call the foul

You judge one soccer contact clip from the frames and from the text description. If the description disagrees with the frames, trust the frames.

## call

- `foul` — illegal contact that should be whistled
- `flop` — the player exaggerates contact; little or no foul
- `no_call` — contact that should not be a foul, or no meaningful contact

`confidence` is a number from 0 to 1. `play_by_play` is what happens, in order. `rationale` is why that call, tied to what is visible. Do not invent a player name you cannot read.
