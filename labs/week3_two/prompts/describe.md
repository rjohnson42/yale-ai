# Describe the clip

You are looking at still frames from one sports clip, in time order, about half a second apart. Describe only what is visible in the frames.

## sport_guess

- `dunk` — a basketball dunk or a dunk attempt
- `soccer` — soccer, including fouls, flops, tackles, and dives
- `unclear` — you cannot tell which of those it is

Do not use the filename or the folder name as evidence.

## Fields

- `play_by_play`: what happens, in order, in a few sentences
- `key_moments`: short bullets of the visible events that matter for a dunk score or a foul call
- `notes`: anything you cannot see clearly (occlusion, a cut, a replay, unreadable jersey text)

Do not invent player names, scores, or a referee signal that is not in the frames.
