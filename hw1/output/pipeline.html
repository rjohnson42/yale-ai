LLM vs Python-only

  • LLM: read_receipts.py, read_bank.py, read_card.py, reconcile.py (Portkey, GPT-5.6-luna, prompt files under prompts/).
  • No LLM: income_statement.py (sums included rows), report.py (HTML), Problem 6 judgment_calls.json (written by analysis, not a script).

Path to income_statement.html

  • Document pack PDFs and emails are extracted into three JSON files, then reconciled against the same pack.
  • The log plus remaining extract lines become income_statement_jan2026.json.
  • report.py joins that statement, the log (excluded rows), and judgment_calls.json into income_statement.html.
